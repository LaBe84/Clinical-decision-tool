CRG TRIAGE

Source material
A contact-pattern analysis identifying a sustained 3-week change in this
caller's contact behaviour (declining frequency, calls ending abruptly before
exploration), plus the existing formulation, built around chronic low mood
managed through weekly supportive contact.

What decision is actually pending
Whether the current formulation still accounts for what's happening, or
whether the pattern now requires a shared re-formulation / material change of
approach — that is a decision about the plan itself, not something the next
contact can resolve through more supportive listening at the current level of
care.

Why the surrounding features don't settle it
The case involves an unresolved, somewhat uncertain pattern and two prior
attempts that didn't produce an explanation — none of that, by itself, is
what justifies CRG. Two failed exploration attempts are not a quota being met;
they are supporting evidence that the change is persistent rather than a
one-off dip. The actual justification is narrower and different: a 3-week
sustained change (declining frequency, calls ending abruptly before
exploration can happen) is a material shift in contact behaviour that the
existing formulation — built around chronic low mood managed through weekly
supportive contact — does not explain. Nothing here is an acute safety
concern, so this isn't about risk severity either; it's that the model
currently guiding the intervention no longer fits the pattern in front of it.

Destination
CRG

Therefore
Because the sustained change is unexplained by the current formulation and
supportive contact at the existing level of care has not produced new
understanding of it, a shared review of the formulation and possible change
of approach is needed — which is what CRG is for, not an emergency response,
so the immediate pathway does not apply, and not a same-shift coordination
question, so TL/Safety Huddle does not apply either.
