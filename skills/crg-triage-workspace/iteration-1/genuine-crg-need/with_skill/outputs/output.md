CRG TRIAGE

Source material
A contact pattern analysis identifying a sustained 3-week change (declining
contact frequency, calls ending abruptly before exploration), unexplained
after two direct attempts to explore it, set against an existing formulation
built around chronic low mood managed through weekly supportive contact.

What decision is actually pending
Whether to continue the current supportive-contact approach when the
formulation it rests on no longer accounts for the caller's behaviour — this
is a decision about revising the shared formulation and/or the model of
intervention itself, which is not something a single Helpline contact can
resolve, since three separate contacts have already tried and produced no
new understanding.

Why the surrounding features don't settle it
The case involves an unexplained pattern (uncertainty) and a formulation that
may no longer fit (a form of complexity), but neither of those, by itself,
is why this is escalating. Uncertainty alone would stay at Helpline. What
does the justificatory work here is narrower: repeated, structured attempts
to test the current formulation (two direct exploration attempts, three
contacts total) have failed to produce any new understanding, meaning the
current level of care has exhausted its own means of resolving the question.
There is no indication of acute risk, so this is not being routed on
seriousness or anxiety about the case — no immediate safety concern is
present, and that absence is treated as such, not read as reassurance that
nothing further is needed.

Destination
CRG

Therefore
Because the existing formulation no longer adequately explains a sustained,
unexplained change and repeated Helpline-level attempts to resolve that gap
have not worked, a shared formulation review is the pending decision, and
that decision belongs to CRG, not to continued Helpline management or an
urgent pathway.
