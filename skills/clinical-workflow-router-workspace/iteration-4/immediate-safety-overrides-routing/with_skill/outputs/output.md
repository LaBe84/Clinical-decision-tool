CLINICAL WORKFLOW ROUTING

Immediate safety check
This describes an acute safety concern requiring action now: the caller reports having just taken a large number of tablets, is becoming drowsy, and is alone. This is an active, in-progress medical emergency, not a routing question. Organisational immediate-risk/emergency procedures take precedence and apply now, independent of and ahead of any skill routing decision below. Routing resumes only once the immediate situation is being handled through those emergency procedures — this skill does not prescribe what to say, whether to keep the caller talking, or how to obtain their location; that belongs to organisational procedure and clinical judgement in the moment, not to this skill's output.

What's actually being asked
On its surface this reads as "what's the right process for handling this case," which could invite a routing answer (e.g. straight to Suicide Enquiry or CRG Triage). But the honest first answer is that nobody is waiting on a skill selection right now — the actual requirement is that emergency procedure engages immediately, given active means already taken and a time-critical physical safety risk (drowsiness alone, unsupervised).

Routing decision
None of the five skills — Suicide Enquiry, PORF Formulation, Contact Pattern Analysis, CRG Triage, CMP/Access Review — are the right process for this moment. The immediate safety override applies and supersedes routing entirely while the emergency is active.

Once the immediate situation has been handled through emergency procedure (and only then, if follow-on clinical reasoning is needed), routing could revisit this material — for example Suicide Enquiry to establish what is known about the event, feeding PORF Formulation for what it means going forward. But that is a separate, later step, not something to decide now.

What each stage needs from the previous one
