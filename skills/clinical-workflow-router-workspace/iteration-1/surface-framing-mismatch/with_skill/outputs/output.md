enquiry or review has been done on it yet, so nothing here has established

Routing decision
Single skill for now: Suicide Enquiry.

Suicide Enquiry is the right first stage because the manager has handed
over raw call material, not an existing enquiry or formulation. Suicide
Enquiry's job is to establish what has actually been established about
suicidality from the material available — including indirect or implicit
content, since suicide was never named explicitly on this call. That
absence of an explicit disclosure does not mean the enquiry is
unnecessary or trivial; it means the enquiry needs to look at what was
said (risk factors, warning signs, changes in functioning, hopelessness,
access to means, goodbye-type language, etc.) rather than searching the
transcript for a keyword that isn't there.

A second stage, PORF Formulation, is very likely needed after that — the
manager's actual question ("is this caller high risk?") is a formulation
question, not an enquiry question, and Suicide Enquiry is deliberately
built not to answer it. But PORF Formulation cannot be run yet: it needs
Suicide Enquiry's output as its input. Naming that dependency now, rather
than jumping to it, is the point of routing this as a sequence rather than
picking whichever skill's topic sounds closest to "risk."

Sequence:
1. Suicide Enquiry — work through the raw transcript and establish what is
   actually known, organized by CASE chronology, including implicit/
   indirect risk-relevant content given that suicide was never named
   outright.
2. PORF Formulation — once 1 exists, use it to determine what the
   presentation means for this caller and what response is proportionate.
   This is the stage that can actually answer "is this caller high risk?"
   — Suicide Enquiry's output alone cannot.

What each stage needs from the previous one
Stage 2 (PORF Formulation) requires Stage 1's (Suicide Enquiry's) CASE-
organized findings as its input — specifically, whatever the enquiry
establishes about current presentation, prior history, and any risk/
protective factors identified in the call, whether or not suicide was
named explicitly. Without that material, a formulation would have nothing
to formulate from, and would end up manufacturing a risk judgement from
the surface transcript instead of from an actual enquiry.
