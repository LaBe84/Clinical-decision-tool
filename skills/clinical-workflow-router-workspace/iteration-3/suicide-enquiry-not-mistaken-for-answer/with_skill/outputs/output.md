CLINICAL WORKFLOW ROUTING

Immediate safety check
Nothing in the material describes an acute safety concern requiring action in the next few minutes (no active means in hand, no attempt in progress). The counsellor has finished the enquiry conversation and is now asking about next steps for the case, not reporting an unfolding emergency. Routing proceeds. (If anything about the specific content of what was disclosed does in fact indicate an immediate risk — e.g. an attempt is currently in progress or means are currently in hand — that takes precedence over everything below and organisational immediate-risk/emergency procedures apply now, independent of this routing.)

What's actually being asked
The counsellor has directly asked the caller about suicidal thoughts, plan, and history, and has written it all down. The question is what should happen next with this case — i.e. what does this documented material mean for this person, and what response is proportionate now.

This is not a question about whether the enquiry was done — the material states plainly that it was completed and documented. There's no indication in what's presented that any domain was skipped, that answers were partial or contradictory, or that completeness is actually in doubt. A routing summary not itemising every CASE component individually is not the same as the underlying enquiry having omitted it, so that's not treated as a gap here.

Routing decision
Single skill: PORF Formulation.

The suicide-disclosure pattern applies here in its simplest form — enquiry has already happened and been documented (Suicide Enquiry's job is done), and the open question is what that material means and what response is proportionate now. That is PORF Formulation's question, not Suicide Enquiry's to answer again, and not something this router resolves itself.

What each stage needs from the previous one
Not applicable — only one skill is involved. PORF Formulation takes the documented CASE material (thoughts, plan, history, as directly established through the enquiry) as its input and determines what it means for this person and what response is proportionate.
