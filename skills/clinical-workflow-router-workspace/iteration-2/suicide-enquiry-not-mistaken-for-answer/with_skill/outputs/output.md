CLINICAL WORKFLOW ROUTING

Immediate safety check
Nothing in what's been presented describes an acute, unfolding safety concern
(active means in hand, an attempt in progress, action needed in the next few
minutes). No override applies on the information given. If anything in the
counsellor's notes does in fact indicate that level of urgency, that takes
precedence over everything below, immediately and independent of this
routing — say so now rather than proceeding through the steps that follow.

What's actually being asked
The counsellor has directly asked about suicidal thoughts, plan, and history
and recorded the answers, and wants to know what should happen next with the
case. The underlying question isn't "was suicide asked about" — that's
already done. It's really two separate questions that are easy to collapse
into one: (1) is what's been recorded actually sufficient material to work
with, and (2) what does this mean for the caller and what response is
proportionate. Only the second of those is a formulation question, and it
can't be answered yet.

Routing decision
Route to Suicide Enquiry first — not as a formality, but because "thoughts,
plan, and history were asked about" tells us direct enquiry occurred, not
that the CASE material is complete. Thoughts, plan, and history are only part
of what CASE-organised enquiry covers; recent trajectory, means, timeframe,
intent, preparatory behaviour, and the anticipated next period aren't
mentioned as having been asked about here, and nothing in the request
confirms they were covered. Suicide Enquiry should be used to organise what
has actually been established, by CASE chronology, from what the counsellor
has written down — and, just as importantly, to make explicit which CASE
elements are still missing rather than letting the fact that *something* was
asked stand in for the enquiry being complete.

PORF Formulation is the second stage, but only once Suicide Enquiry's output
shows the CASE material is actually sufficient to formulate from. If Suicide
Enquiry's output surfaces gaps (e.g. no timeframe, no means, no sense of
recent trajectory or intent), the next step is completing that enquiry with
the caller — not proceeding to PORF on a partial picture. Do not treat this
as a fixed two-step pipeline that runs automatically; whether PORF is needed
next depends entirely on what Suicide Enquiry's output actually establishes.

What each stage needs from the previous one
Suicide Enquiry needs the counsellor's recorded material (what was asked,
what was answered) as raw input, and produces a CASE-organised account of
what is and isn't established. PORF Formulation, if and when it's reached,
needs that CASE-organised account — specifically, a sufficient picture across
the relevant CASE elements, not just the thoughts/plan/history subset — as
its input; it should not be started on the raw notes directly, and it should
not be started on a CASE account that Suicide Enquiry itself has flagged as
incomplete.

Not routed to
- CMP/Access Review — nothing here indicates an existing or proposed access
  arrangement is in question.
- Contact Pattern Analysis — this isn't a contact-frequency or engagement-
  pattern case; it's a single enquiry needing organisation and, potentially,
  completion.
- CRG Triage — no unresolved decision about escalation or level of care has
  been identified yet, and none should be assumed just because suicidality is
  involved. Whether CRG Triage becomes relevant depends on what Suicide
  Enquiry (and PORF, if reached) actually establish — it isn't earned by the
  topic alone.
