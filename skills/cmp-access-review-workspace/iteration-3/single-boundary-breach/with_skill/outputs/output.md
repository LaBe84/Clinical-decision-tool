CMP / ACCESS REVIEW

What is happening
The caller is under an informal agreement to contact only during evening hours. In the six months this arrangement has been in place, they have contacted outside the agreed window once: a single call at 2pm, during which the caller was clearly distressed about an acute daytime event. This is the only recorded departure from the agreed contact window in that period.

Function of current access arrangement
Not established from the material provided. No formulation or contact pattern analysis is referenced here, so the clinical purpose the evening-hours restriction was originally set up to serve is not stated. This gap is noted rather than filled by inference.

Is the current intervention working
Six months of adherence to the agreed window is a behavioural fact about workability — it shows the caller can and does follow the arrangement. It does not, on its own, establish that the arrangement is clinically effective or proportionate; that would require its own evidence, which isn't presented here. Separately, the single out-of-window contact needs to be considered on its own terms, not folded into a workability judgement either way: it was one contact, in a context distinct from the pattern the agreement addresses (an acute daytime event prompting a distressed call), against a six-month history of otherwise consistent adherence. That is not evidence of failure, misuse, or erosion of the agreement, and it does not indicate the arrangement is unworkable or breaking down. This review does not need to, and does not, characterise the call itself as appropriate or warranted — that is not a judgement this process is positioned to make. What can be said is narrower: this one contextually distinct exception does not change the assessment of whether the arrangement is working.

Decision required
Continue current access — the existing informal agreement continues as is. Nothing in the material presented supports modifying, tightening, or formalising the arrangement on the basis of this single contact.

Proposed change (if any)
No change proposed.

Safety pathway note
Nothing in this review restricts or delays response to immediate safety concerns. The caller's contact during an acute daytime event was responded to; that response is not in question here and this review does not generate any follow-on referral or restriction as a result of the contact occurring outside the agreed window.
