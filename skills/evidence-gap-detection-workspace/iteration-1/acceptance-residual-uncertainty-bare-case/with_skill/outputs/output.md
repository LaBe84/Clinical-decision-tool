EVIDENCE-GAP ANALYSIS

Decision under examination
Whether anything needs to change about the current intervention (weekly
supportive calls) right now.

Established evidence
- The current intervention is weekly supportive calls (established as the
  ongoing arrangement, PORF: no material change from baseline).
- No complaints have been raised.
- No new concerns have been reported.
- Nothing else has been reported in relation to the case since baseline.

Evidence gaps
- Detailed content of what occurred within each individual call (e.g.
  session-by-session clinical detail beyond "no new concerns") — missing
  (not addressed in the material presented; only the absence of complaints
  or new concerns is documented).
- Any independent corroboration of stability beyond self/caller-reported
  absence of concerns — missing (not assessed; not stated as having been
  checked and found negative, simply not addressed in the material).

Gap classification
1. Detailed session content beyond "no new concerns" — Residual
   uncertainty.
2. Independent corroboration of stability — Residual uncertainty.

Materiality analysis
1. Session-level detail: If this were resolved in either plausible
   direction (i.e. the calls turn out to contain more texture than "no
   concerns," or they turn out to be exactly as bare as reported), the
   decision under examination — whether to change the current weekly-call
   intervention right now — would not change. The stated decision rests on
   whether there is a positive indication to change something, and no such
   indication has been reported in either direction. Resolving this gap
   could add descriptive detail but could not, on its own, supply a reason
   to alter the intervention that isn't already either present or absent in
   the material as given. Residual uncertainty.
2. Independent corroboration: If this were resolved in either plausible
   direction (corroboration obtained and consistent with "no concerns," or
   corroboration obtained and revealing something new), the second branch
   would in fact change the decision — it would introduce a new concern
   where none was previously reported. However, the material presented
   states, as a premise of the scenario (PORF: no material change from
   baseline), that no new concerns and no complaints exist. Absent any
   indication in the material that this reported absence is unreliable,
   contested, or drawn from a source with a plausible reason to be
   incomplete, there is nothing in the presented material to suggest this
   branch is a live possibility rather than a hypothetical one. Under this
   skill's rule against treating every unknown as decision-critical by
   default, and against inferring problems the record does not indicate,
   this is classified as residual uncertainty, not decision-critical: the
   material as presented supports "no reported concerns," and nothing here
   points to that finding being unreliable.

What remains supportable
- The current intervention (weekly supportive calls) can continue as is.
- No change to the intervention is required on the basis of the material
  presented — the decision "no material change from baseline" is supported
  by what has been established (no complaints, no new concerns, nothing
  else reported), and the identified gaps do not undermine that support
  under the counterfactual test.

What is not supportable
- A claim that stability has been positively verified or clinically
  confirmed (as opposed to: nothing adverse has been reported). "No
  complaints, no new concerns" is an absence-of-report finding, not a
  verified-stable finding, and should not be represented as the latter.
- Any claim that the two residual gaps have been ruled out rather than
  simply judged non-material to this decision.

Information requirement
None required to support the decision under examination. If ongoing
monitoring practice calls for periodic independent corroboration of
stability (a policy or practice matter, not a gap this decision depends on
resolving now), that is a matter for whoever owns routine review cadence,
not a precondition for the decision in front of us.

Residual uncertainty
- The fine-grained content of individual calls beyond "no concerns raised."
- Whether the reported absence of complaints/concerns has been independently
  corroborated, as opposed to simply not contradicted.
Both can remain unresolved without blocking the decision that the
intervention continues unchanged.
