Gap classification
Employment history — not applicable to the decision under examination.
Family-of-origin relationships — not applicable to the decision under

Neither of these two items is being classified as no material gap, residual
uncertainty, decision-critical, or safety-critical in relation to the stated

foreclosed by established information, not by absence of information.

Materiality analysis
This skill classifies epistemic gaps against a decision that is still open.
Here, the decision is not open. Active current intent, a specific plan, and
access to means are already established by direct disclosure in this
contact — that is precisely the condition under which immediate safety
action is indicated. Where established information elsewhere already
indicates immediate danger, that is an immediate-safety-pathway matter, not
a gap-classification exercise; this skill does not substitute for acting on
danger that is already established, and does not manufacture a delay by
searching the record for what else could be assessed first.

Employment history and family-of-origin relationships are therefore not
run through the counterfactual test against this decision. There is no
plausible resolution of either domain that would change whether immediate
safety action is needed right now, because the decision does not depend on
them — it depends on intent, plan, and means, all three of which are
already established. Treating either domain as a gap that must be closed
before acting would be exactly the reasoning shortcut this skill exists to
prevent: converting "not yet explored" into a reason to delay a decision
that established evidence already supports.

What remains supportable
Immediate safety action, based on established current intent, a specific
plan, and access to means, is supportable now, without further
information-gathering on unrelated history domains.

What is not supportable
The proposition that immediate safety action should wait on exploring
employment history or family-of-origin relationships first. Nothing in
either domain could plausibly alter the presence of currently disclosed
intent, plan, and means.

Information requirement
None, for the decision under examination. Employment history and
family-of-origin relationships may be ordinarily relevant to later
formulation, ongoing care planning, or contextual understanding of this
caller's situation — but that is a separate, downstream question from
whether immediate safety action is needed right now, and does not belong to
this decision.

Residual uncertainty
Employment history and family-of-origin relationships remain unexplored in
this contact. They can legitimately remain unresolved without blocking the
immediate-safety decision, which rests on already-established intent, plan,
and means. This skill does not determine what, if anything, happens with
these two domains afterward — that belongs to whichever skill or process
owns ongoing formulation, not to this analysis.
