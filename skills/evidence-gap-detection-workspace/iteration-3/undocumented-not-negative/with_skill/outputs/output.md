EVIDENCE-GAP ANALYSIS

Decision under examination
Is it safe to conclude that this caller has no current suicide plan?

Established evidence
The Suicide Enquiry notes document mood, recent stressors, and general
coping. These are genuinely established, by direct assessment within this
contact. Nothing in the notes states, or implies, that plan or intent were
directly asked about at any point in this contact.

Evidence gaps
Whether the caller currently has a suicide plan, and whether current intent
was assessed — missing (never asked/assessed within this contact). The
record does not show this question was put to the caller and does not
record any answer, positive or negative. This is distinct from a finding
that plan/intent were asked about and denied; no such finding is present
here.

Gap classification
Whether plan/intent were directly asked and what the answer was —
Safety-critical gap.

Materiality analysis
Counterfactual test, applied at the decision's actual stated granularity
("is it safe to conclude no current plan"):
- If plan/intent had been asked and the caller denied any plan, that would
  support the conclusion the decision is asking about.
- If plan/intent had been asked and the caller disclosed a plan, that would
  directly contradict the conclusion and require an entirely different
  response.
- Since either plausible answer changes whether the conclusion "no current
  plan" is supportable, the gap passes the counterfactual test and is
  material, not residual.
This is safety-critical rather than merely decision-critical because the
specific decision under examination is itself a safety reliance question —
whether it is safe to conclude no plan exists. The unresolved question is
not adjacent background; it is the exact fact the decision purports to
conclude on. Absence of any signal toward a plan in the mood/stressors/
coping material does not resolve this: those domains were assessed, but
none of them are the domain the decision depends on, so their apparent
reassurance carries no weight here regardless of how thorough they read.

What remains supportable
The notes support conclusions about the caller's mood, recent stressors,
and general coping as documented in this contact.

What is not supportable
That the caller has no current suicide plan cannot be concluded from this
material. The record's silence on plan/intent is missing evidence, not a
negative finding, and cannot be read as reassurance that no plan exists.

Information requirement
Direct assessment of current suicide plan and intent, obtained from the
caller in this contact or the next available contact.

Residual uncertainty
None identified beyond the safety-critical gap above; no other candidate
gap in this material meets the threshold for nomination under the
decision as stated.
