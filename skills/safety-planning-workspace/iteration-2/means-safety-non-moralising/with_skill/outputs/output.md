Means / environmental safety

Access problem: PORF material establishes that the caller has access to a firearm kept at home, and that this access is clinically relevant to their current risk — i.e. it is a lethal means available to them within the environment where their crisis pattern plays out.

Feasible reduction in access: The relevant reduction is removing the firearm from the home environment entirely (temporary off-site storage elsewhere, e.g. with another party, or via a formal storage/surrender option) rather than any in-home modification. In-home measures do not reliably separate the person from access during a crisis and are not treated here as an equivalent substitute for removal.

Who can safely implement it: Not established in the material provided — the plan needs to identify a specific person (not the caller themself, given the access problem is the caller's own access) who is willing and able to take the firearm off-site, or confirm the caller's own willingness to arrange formal temporary storage/surrender through an appropriate service. If no such person or pathway has been identified yet, that is a gap to close as part of finishing this plan, not an assumption to fill in.

Feasibility: Not established in the material provided whether removal is realistic in this case — e.g. whether the caller will consent, whether a person willing to hold the firearm exists and is available in the relevant timeframe, or whether a formal surrender pathway is accessible to them. This needs to be checked directly with the caller rather than assumed either way.

Completion status: Pending. Based on the material provided, this is a means-safety action that has been identified as clinically indicated but not yet actioned — agreement to consider it (if any) is not the same as the firearm actually having left the home. Status should be updated to "removed/actioned" only once that has actually occurred, and followed up rather than left as an open item indefinitely.

If this isn't enough
If the firearm cannot be removed from the home (caller declines, no available person, no accessible pathway) before the next period of risk, this must be escalated as an active safety gap — name it explicitly rather than letting the plan proceed as if means safety were resolved, and route to the appropriate emergency/crisis pathway per the immediate-danger guidance if risk is current.

Collaboration status
Not established in the material provided whether the caller has actively agreed to this step, partially engaged, or not yet been asked — record the actual status once known rather than assuming agreement.

Feasibility check
Untested: whether a specific person or pathway for removal exists and is genuinely available; whether the caller will follow through under actual crisis conditions rather than in a calm conversation. This entire section should be treated as unresolved until those are confirmed.
