# Safety Plan — Immediate Danger Presentation

## Preliminary note (read before the plan itself)

Per the skill boundary: *"It does not override the immediate risk pathway — a
safety plan is not a substitute for responding to current danger."*

The PORF material as described here — active plan, current access to means,
unresolved/concerning intent — describes a person in immediate danger, not a
person approaching a future crisis period. That distinction changes what this
document is for and how it's sequenced:

- This is **not** a standalone deliverable to hand over and end contact on.
  The immediate-risk pathway (direct assessment of current safety, emergency
  services/crisis response as indicated by policy, means restriction in the
  moment) takes precedence over plan-building. If that pathway hasn't already
  been actioned, route there first — this document does not substitute for
  it.
- Because danger is current rather than anticipated, the invariant applies in
  full: **escalation and means-safety content cannot sit at the bottom of a
  five-step document.** They lead. Coping steps and longer-horizon supports
  are secondary and only meaningful once immediate danger has been addressed.
- If the person is not currently collaborating, or collaboration is uncertain
  given the acuity, that must be recorded plainly (see Collaboration status)
  rather than smoothed into an "agreed plan" that didn't actually happen that
  way.

The structure below reflects that ordering. It is a template shaped for this
acuity level — the bracketed content needs the actual PORF/Suicide Enquiry
specifics to be real; nothing here should be filled with placeholders that
read like content (see skill's reasoning-shortcuts list).

## SAFETY PLAN — IMMEDIATE DANGER

### 0. Immediate response status (precedes the plan)
[State plainly whether the immediate-risk pathway has already been actioned
— e.g., emergency services contacted, current physical safety confirmed,
means already partially or fully separated from the person — or whether this
document is being built *before* that has happened, in which case say so and
do not proceed to build out steps 3–5 as if the situation were stable.]

### 1. If this isn't enough — escalation (moved first, not last)
[Explicit, specific, immediately actionable: who is contacted, how, right
now, if risk escalates further or the plan doesn't hold in the next few
minutes/hours — not "call us if it gets worse" as the whole of this section.
Name the specific service/number relevant to jurisdiction and policy, name a
specific person if one is genuinely available right now, and state what
"this isn't working" looks like for this person specifically, drawn from the
existing formulation (e.g. specific escalation cues already identified in
PORF/Suicide Enquiry material — not a generic deterioration marker).]

### 2. Means / environmental safety (elevated ahead of coping steps)
[Because access to means is current and specific per the PORF material,
concrete separation steps come before internal coping content, not after.
State exactly what the means is, who can take custody of it or whether
removal from the immediate environment is being done directly, by whom, and
by when — "by end of this contact" if acuity requires it, not "this week."
Frame as practical risk reduction, not a test of intent. If no one is
confirmed available to hold/remove the means, say that plainly rather than
listing an unconfirmed person as if the step is complete.]

### 3. Crisis state this plan addresses
[Specific, drawn from existing formulation/enquiry material — the exact
pattern already established, not a generic "if things get bad again."]

### 4. Early warning signs
[Person-specific, early enough to act on — included for continuity/future
use, but note explicitly that if the current state has already progressed
past these cues, steps 1–2 above are what's operative right now, not this
section.]

### 5. Independent coping steps
[Only include what's actually feasible in the current state per the
feasibility check below. If the person's current presentation (intoxication,
dissociation, acuity) makes independent coping steps unrealistic right now,
say so rather than listing steps that read well but won't be used.]

### 6. Interpersonal / environmental support
[Specific people, specific contact method, with availability and willingness
at this actual time noted or flagged as unknown — not named because they
exist in the person's life. If a named support person is unavailable or is
implicated in the crisis pattern per the formulation, do not list them here.]

### Collaboration status
[Record accurately: did the person actively collaborate on this plan, engage
partially, or were they unable/unwilling to engage given current acuity? If
this document was built without full collaboration because immediate risk
took precedence, state that — do not present it as an agreed plan if it
isn't one.]

### Feasibility check
[What in this plan is tested against the person's actual crisis state
(memory, access, shame, intoxication, isolation, time of day, interpersonal
conflict) versus what remains genuinely unknown. Given the acuity here, flag
explicitly if steps 3–6 are aspirational/for-later rather than validated for
the current moment — the plan should not overstate its own reliability at
this stage.]

## Why the structure differs from a standard (future-crisis) plan

Standard ordering (crisis state → early warning signs → independent coping →
interpersonal support → means safety → escalation) assumes the plan is being
built *ahead of* a crisis period, for later use. Here, per the material
described, indicators point to danger that is current, not anticipated:
active plan, present access to means, intent not resolved. Under the skill's
invariant, that reorders the document — escalation and means safety move to
the top and are treated as the operative content, with the remaining sections
retained for continuity but explicitly marked as secondary to what happens in
this contact. This is a sequencing and emphasis change, not a different
skill: the six operational questions are all still answered, just not in the
default order, and not as if the situation were already stable enough for
step 3 onward to be the priority.
