Risk Status

Risk State
A within-person comparison requires a baseline for this specific person, and
none exists: this is the first contact on file, so there is no prior
presentation to compare tonight's calls against. The six calls in 48 hours are
therefore acute-window data, not a change from a known starting point. Within
that window, the content is inconsistent — two of six calls reference
"thinking about ending it," four do not — and each call has ended shortly
after the coping question is raised. This pattern cannot, on its own, be read
as escalation, de-escalation, ambivalence, testing-the-service, or reassurance
seeking; the function of the repeated, truncated contact is not established
from frequency or from the pattern of hang-ups alone. It is equally true that
the ideation comments are not established as resolved, minimised, or
withdrawn — they have not been retracted, only not repeated in four of six
calls, and non-mention is not evidence of absence. Whether intent, planning,
or capability has changed at any point across the six calls cannot currently
be determined.

Foreseeable Change
Plausible developments include: a future call in which the coping question is
answered rather than avoided, which would be the first point at which direct
suicide enquiry could actually be completed; a call in which she discloses
current intent, a plan, or access to means, which would materially change the

current uncertainty rather than resolve it. Cessation of calling altogether is
also foreseeable; this would represent loss of visibility into her situation,
not, by itself, evidence that her risk has increased or decreased.

is prompting the pattern of repeated brief contact, why calls end when coping

materially uncertain. No historical or collateral information exists to

Integrated formulation
Because there is no prior history and no collateral information, enduring
vulnerability cannot be assessed against any reference population, and there
is no established baseline against which to judge whether tonight's pattern
represents a change for this person. Currently, six brief calls in 48 hours
show inconsistent mention of suicidal ideation and a repeated pattern of
disengagement at the point coping is asked about; this is acute-window
information, not a confirmed deterioration or a confirmed resolution.
However, repeated contact with the service is itself an observable point of
engagement, though nothing about what other resources she has, or is able to
use, is currently confirmed. The main uncertainties are current intent,
planning, means access, and the function of the repeated short calls, none
of which can be resolved from call frequency or content pattern alone. The
formulation would be materially changed by a call in which the coping
question is answered, by disclosure of intent or a plan, or by information
narrowing why calls repeatedly end at the same point. Therefore, the
proportionate response is to prioritise completing direct suicide enquiry
during the next contact, rather than to escalate on frequency or pattern
alone, which the formulation does not support as an independent risk
indicator.

Therefore
Continued Helpline management with a specific focus on completing direct
suicide enquiry at next contact is the response the formulation supports;
escalation is not currently supported because the basis available (call
frequency, truncated calls, inconsistent historical mentions of ideation) has
been assessed and found not to establish current risk state, intent, or
motivation on its own, and no other information supports a higher-tier
response at this time.
