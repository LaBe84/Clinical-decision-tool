INTERVENTION SELECTION

Target
Reduce immediate suicide risk. Suicide Enquiry has established that the caller has taken steps toward suicide right now and is at immediate risk — this is the only target this skill will state. It is not extended into any broader treatment target (e.g. long-term risk reduction, engagement with a care pathway, contact-pattern change), because PORF, Contact Pattern Analysis, and CRG Triage have not yet been completed and offer nothing this skill can draw on.

Established basis
Established upstream finding: Suicide Enquiry has determined the caller has taken active steps toward suicide and is at immediate risk. This is treated as established fact, not hypothesis, and is not re-formulated here. No other upstream finding is available or required to support this target — immediate danger is sufficient on its own.

Selected response
Destination 7 — Immediate safety action. This bypasses the rest of the decision process rather than competing with it; the incomplete status of PORF, Contact Pattern Analysis, and CRG Triage does not delay it and is not a precondition for it.

Mechanism
We are initiating immediate safety action now because we expect it to influence the caller's immediate physical safety through the applicable emergency-response protocol for active, in-progress danger — not through any of the longer-term mechanisms (regulation, engagement, contact-pattern change) that would depend on the still-incomplete formulation work.

Proportionality
Established immediate danger is the maximum-intensity trigger this skill recognises; an immediate safety action is the response proportionate to that finding, not an escalation beyond what's established. No lower-intensity response is proportionate once immediate danger is established, and no additional intensity beyond immediate safety action is indicated by anything established so far.

Risks / unintended effects
Not evaluated here and not a reason to delay: boundary rules are explicit that immediate safety action must not be delayed when established information indicates immediate danger. Longer-term risks (dependency, fragmented ownership, undermining an existing plan) are out of scope for this response and would only become relevant to any subsequent, separate response once the acute period passes and the rest of the workflow can inform it.

Ownership
The applicable organisational emergency-response protocol for immediate danger owns execution of this action. Once the acute period has passed, ownership of what happens next returns to Clinical Workflow Router to determine whether PORF, Contact Pattern Analysis, and CRG Triage should now be completed — this skill does not instruct that those be run, only names that the workflow is incomplete and routes back.

Review
We will know this is helping if the immediate danger is resolved and the caller is safe. We will reconsider if the immediate-danger indicators persist despite the action taken. Review timing is set by the applicable emergency-response protocol, not by this skill; once the acute period has passed, the case should return to the router for completion of the outstanding workflow steps before any further intervention (beyond the immediate safety action) is selected.

Residual uncertainty
PORF, Contact Pattern Analysis, and CRG Triage remain incomplete. Nothing about the caller's broader formulation, contact pattern, or triage destination is established, and no target or response beyond the immediate safety action should be inferred from this material. Once the acute period passes, this skill will need to be re-run with the completed upstream findings before any further intervention (maintain, modify, add, reduce/stop, or escalate) is selected.
