INTERVENTION SELECTION

Target
Not yet established. No PORF conclusion, contact pattern finding, or
specific unmet need has been identified — only a shared staff sense of
unease. "Something needs to be done" names a felt pressure to act, not a
target for action. Without a stated target, no intervention can be selected
without being ritual rather than treatment.

Established basis
None available at the level this skill requires. What exists is: multiple
staff independently reporting concern ("we all agree something needs to be
done here"). That is worth taking seriously as a signal that something in
the case may warrant closer attention — but it is not itself an established
upstream finding (no PORF conclusion), not an established contact-pattern
finding, and not a stated or inferred unmet need. It should be named
explicitly as a staff-observation signal, not upgraded into a clinical
finding.

Selected response
Destination 6 applies: seek additional information before selecting an
intervention. Nothing is proposed yet. The consensus among staff is real and
should be recorded and taken seriously, but it does not, by itself, indicate
which — if any — intervention is warranted.

Mechanism
Not applicable. No target has been established, so no causal "we are doing
X because we expect it to influence Y through Z" sentence can be completed.
Attempting to complete it now would mean reverse-engineering a mechanism to
justify an action already decided on, which this skill must not do.

Proportionality
Not assessable yet — proportionality is judged against an established need,
and none has been established here. The strength of staff consensus is not
a measure of clinical need and must not be used as a proxy for it.

Risks / unintended effects
Selecting an intervention now, before a target exists, risks: manufacturing
a treatment target from generalised unease; adding activity that increases
service contact without a defined purpose (which itself risks reinforcing
patterns not yet analysed); creating the appearance that something has been
addressed when nothing about the underlying concern has actually been
clarified; and diverting attention from the real next step, which is
finding out what, specifically, the unease is about.

Ownership
Route back to Clinical Workflow Router to determine what upstream
work — establishing current risk state, contact-pattern function, or
whatever the specific missing element turns out to be — is needed to give
this shared concern a nameable target. This skill does not itself dispatch
PORF, Suicide Enquiry, Contact Pattern Analysis, or CRG Triage.

Review
Not applicable to an intervention, since none is selected. What should be
reviewed is the information gap itself: once upstream work clarifies
whether there is a specific finding (risk-state change, contact-pattern
concern, unmet need, or something else), intervention selection can be
re-run against that established basis.

Residual uncertainty
What, specifically, is driving staff unease has not been articulated in
terms this skill can act on. It is unknown whether this reflects an
emerging risk change, a contact-pattern shift, an unmet need, or a
service-level anxiety not yet tied to the individual case. That gap is the
next thing to resolve — not a reason to select an intervention in its
absence.
