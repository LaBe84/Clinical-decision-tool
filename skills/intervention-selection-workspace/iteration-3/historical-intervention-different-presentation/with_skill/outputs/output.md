INTERVENTION SELECTION

Target
Not yet established. "Reduce distress in this caller" is not a sufficient
target statement. What would need to be stated is the specific mechanism
driving the current presentation — e.g. intrusive-thought-related distress,
agitation/hyperarousal following bereavement, avoidance of grief-related
cues, or something else — and that has not been supplied. The target that
was established two years ago (low-energy withdrawal secondary to
depression following job loss) is not the target now. Nothing in the
material establishes what the current target is.

Established basis
What we actually know: (1) two years ago, a structured behavioural
activation (BA) plan was associated with improvement during a depressive
episode marked by low energy and withdrawal following job loss; (2) the
caller is now in crisis again, triggered by bereavement, presenting with
agitation and intrusive thoughts. That the same person had a prior crisis
and a prior intervention that helped is established. That the same
intervention is indicated now is not — it is an inference, not a finding,
and the presentation described is materially different from the one BA was
originally matched to.

Selected response
Destination 6 — seek additional information before selecting an
intervention. The proposal to reuse the behavioural activation plan is not
ready to be adopted or rejected outright on the mechanism question, because
the current target has not been established. Before any intervention is
selected, what's needed is a current formulation of what is driving the
agitation and intrusive thoughts in this bereavement-triggered crisis, and
what immediate risk, if any, is present. That is upstream work (current
risk-state finding, current formulation of function/target), not something
this skill backfills or infers from the old plan.

Mechanism
Cannot be completed. Behavioural activation's mechanism was: "we do
structured activity scheduling because we expect it to counteract
avoidance/withdrawal and low behavioural reinforcement, which was
maintaining the depressive presentation." That causal chain does not
transfer to agitation and intrusive thoughts, which are not established to
be maintained by behavioural withdrawal at all — they may reflect a
different process entirely (e.g. hyperarousal, unprocessed grief-related
cognitions, threat appraisal). Asserting BA's mechanism would apply here
without that being established would be upgrading a hypothesis
("BA generally helps this person") into a stated basis for action, which
this skill does not do.

Proportionality
Cannot be assessed yet, because proportionality is judged against a stated
target, and no target is established for the current presentation.

Risks / unintended effects
If the BA plan were reused now without re-establishing the target: it risks
being mechanistically mismatched to agitation and intrusive thoughts (doing
something with no plausible causal path to the actual presenting problem);
it risks the caller and team mistaking "we did the thing that worked before"
for "we did the thing this crisis needs," which delays whatever the correct
response actually is; and it risks reinforcing a general pattern of pattern-
matching interventions to history rather than to current formulation, which
degrades intervention selection more broadly. There is also a narrower risk
specific to structured activity scheduling in acute grief with agitation:
if intrusive thoughts or agitation are being driven by something that needs
containment or processing rather than activation, a generic activation
schedule could add pressure/structure at a moment where that isn't what's
indicated — but this is a risk to flag, not a conclusion to assert, since
the current formulation isn't established either way.

Ownership
Route back to whatever produces current formulation for this presentation
(current risk-state / target-defining assessment for this crisis) before
an intervention is selected. Once that target is established, return to
this skill to select the intervention.

Review
Not applicable yet — no intervention has been selected. Once a target and
mechanism are established, a review criterion (what "helping" looks like,
what would trigger reconsideration, and a review date) must be set at that
point.

Residual uncertainty
Whether the prior BA plan, or some component of it, could be relevant again
is genuinely unknown — it isn't ruled out, only not yet justified. What
remains unknown: what is functionally driving the agitation and intrusive
thoughts in this grief presentation, whether there is any immediate safety
concern requiring action ahead of this process, and whether the caller's
own preference or read of what previously helped should factor into the
eventual selection once a target is established.

---
Note on the team member's reasoning: "it worked last time" reaches for a
historically effective intervention without checking whether the current
presentation is materially the same as when it worked — it isn't (different
trigger, different symptom profile, different presumed mechanism). A
documented history of use, or of prior benefit, is not itself a current
justification. That doesn't mean BA is wrong — it means the question hasn't
been asked yet, and needs to be before any plan is reused.
