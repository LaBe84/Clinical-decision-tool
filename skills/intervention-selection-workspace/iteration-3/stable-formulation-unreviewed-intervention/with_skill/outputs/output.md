INTERVENTION SELECTION

Target
Not currently established for the existing intervention. The fortnightly check-in calls have been running for eighteen months with no record of a stated target (e.g. what they were meant to change) and no review against one. Separately, PORF's finding — stable presentation relative to baseline, no material change in Risk State, no new plan/intent — establishes only that there is no basis to escalate. It does not itself supply, or substitute for, a target for the existing intervention.

Established basis
- Established upstream finding (PORF): presentation stable, no material Risk State change, no new plan/intent. This is a finding about escalation, not about the check-in calls' effectiveness.
- Established fact (case record): the fortnightly check-in intervention was set up eighteen months ago and has never been reviewed against a target.
- Not established: what the check-ins were intended to achieve, whether they are achieving it, whether the current frequency/format is still warranted, or whether stopping/modifying them would have any effect on risk or engagement.

Selected response
Maintain the current fortnightly check-in calls for now — the available material gives no basis to change them today — combined with routing the missing piece back for action: an overdue review that establishes a target, mechanism, and review criterion for this intervention, since none has ever been set. Nothing about stopping, intensifying, or modifying the calls is proposed at this time; that decision is deferred until the missing target/review exists.

Mechanism
Not completable for the existing intervention as currently understood — there is no stated target, so no causal sentence ("we are doing X because we expect it to influence Y through Z") can honestly be written for it. This gap is exactly what the overdue review needs to resolve; manufacturing a mechanism now to justify continuation would violate the rule against retrofitting a treatment target from uncertainty.

Proportionality
Cannot be assessed against a target that doesn't exist. What can be said: PORF's stable finding removes any basis for a more intensive response, and nothing in the material establishes harm from continuing the calls unchanged in the meantime. That supports not changing anything abruptly today — it does not support a claim that fortnightly contact is the right intensity.

Risks / unintended effects
- Continuing indefinitely without ever establishing a target risks the intervention becoming ritual rather than treatment, and risks fostering dependency on a contact pattern whose purpose was never defined.
- Stopping or reducing it right now, absent any review, would carry the mirror risk: withdrawing a fixed feature of the person's contact with the service without evidence either that it's unneeded or that it's safe to remove.
- Treating "presentation is stable" as proof the check-ins are working would be mistaking a stable pattern for an effective intervention — stability could equally reflect factors unrelated to the calls.

Ownership
The overdue review of this intervention's target and effect sits with the case's clinical review process / responsible clinician, not with this skill. If, once a target is established, the current approach turns out not to be achieving it, that becomes a fresh run of this skill (destination 2: modify) or a decision to stop (destination 4) — not a decision to make now on incomplete information.

Review
This intervention should be formally reviewed — target stated, mechanism specified, and a review criterion (we will know it's helping if...; we will reconsider if...; we will review by...) set — at the next scheduled clinical review, and in any case no later than a defined near-term date given eighteen months have already elapsed without one. Until that review happens, "continue unchanged" is a holding position, not an endorsement.

Residual uncertainty
Whether the fortnightly check-in calls are achieving anything, whether their frequency is still appropriate, whether the person finds them helpful or burdensome, and whether an alternative or reduced arrangement would serve the same purpose — none of this is established by the current material and none of it is resolved by PORF's stable-presentation finding.
