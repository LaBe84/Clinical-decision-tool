INTERVENTION SELECTION

Target
Reduce immediate suicide risk / prevent loss of life right now. This is the
only target that can and needs to be stated at this point — it is not a
placeholder pending the rest of the workflow, it is the actual established
target.

Established basis
Suicide Enquiry has established that the caller has taken steps toward
suicide right now and is at immediate risk. That single finding is
sufficient basis for action on its own. PORF, Contact Pattern Analysis, and
CRG Triage have not been completed, and none of them are needed to justify
this response — per the boundary rules, immediate safety action must not be
delayed when established information indicates immediate danger, and
Suicide Enquiry's conclusion is not overwritten or second-guessed by waiting
for the rest of the case workflow.

Selected response
Destination 7 — Immediate safety action. This bypasses the rest of the
decision process rather than competing with it: no proportionality
weighing, no least-burdensome calibration, no waiting on PORF/Contact
Pattern Analysis/CRG Triage. Concretely this means engaging the
organisation's mandatory immediate-risk protocol now — keeping the caller
engaged, obtaining location/means information as the protocol directs,
initiating emergency service contact (or organisational equivalent) per
policy, and not closing or downgrading the call pending completion of the
rest of the case workflow.

Mechanism
We are acting immediately because we expect it to prevent death or injury
through direct interruption of an act already in progress — this is not a
mechanism about future risk reduction, engagement, or coping capacity; it is
about stopping physical harm that is happening now. No more elaborate causal
account is needed or appropriate at this decision point.

Proportionality
Maximum proportionate response given imminent danger to life. The six
decision domains (target/mechanism/proportionality/least-burdensome/
contraindications/review) that normally structure this skill's output are
explicitly not being run in full here — per the legitimate destinations
list, immediate safety action bypasses that process rather than being
weighed against lesser options. Asking whether something smaller would
suffice is itself the error this destination exists to prevent.

Risks / unintended effects
Named, not resolved: emergency service involvement may have consequences
the caller experiences as adversarial (loss of autonomy, police contact,
possible involuntary intervention); acting without PORF/Contact Pattern
Analysis/CRG Triage means the response is not informed by full contextual
history, prior response to intervention, or known preferences, which could
affect how the emergency response is best shaped. These are real costs, but
none of them justify delay — they are matters for follow-up once immediate
danger is addressed, not conditions on acting now.

Ownership
Call handler/duty worker currently on the contact: execute organisational
immediate-risk protocol now. Supervisor/on-call clinical lead: notified
immediately per policy and available for the emergency-service liaison if
required. Whoever holds the case after the immediate action concludes owns
completing the deferred workflow (see Review).

Review
We will know this is helping if the immediate danger is interrupted (caller
remains engaged/safe, or emergency responders reach the caller). We will
reconsider the approach if the protocol itself cannot be executed as
designed (e.g., contact is lost, location cannot be established) — at that
point escalate further per policy rather than downgrading. We will review by
treating this as immediate and time-critical: not on a scheduled interval,
but the moment the immediate-danger episode is resolved, at which point
PORF, Contact Pattern Analysis, and CRG Triage must be completed before any
further intervention decision (destinations 1–6) is made — none of that
work is skipped, only deferred until safety is no longer the acute concern.

Residual uncertainty
Everything the unfinished workflow would normally establish remains unknown:
overall risk formulation (PORF), whether this contact fits a wider pattern
(Contact Pattern Analysis), and appropriate ongoing triage destination (CRG
Triage). None of that uncertainty is resolved by this decision, and none of
it needs to be resolved before acting — it is explicitly carried forward as
outstanding work for once the immediate-danger episode concludes, not
backfilled or guessed at here.
