Mechanism
Not applicable — there is no proposed intervention to attach a mechanism to. Naming a mechanism now would mean inventing a causal story to justify action the case doesn't yet support, which is the exact failure mode this skill exists to block.

Proportionality
Not applicable for the same reason. Proportionality is evaluated against an established need; there isn't one on the table yet. The proportionate response to unease-without-a-target is diagnostic (go find the target), not therapeutic (do something to the case).

Risks / unintended effects
Risks of what *not* to do here matter more than risks of a specific intervention, since none is being selected:
- Manufacturing a treatment target from staff discomfort rather than from evidence, and then intervening against that manufactured target.
- Selecting a "safe-sounding" default (e.g., increased check-ins, a new safety plan review, an access change) that reassures staff and looks like action but is not tied to any established finding — this can create dependency, reinforce contact patterns, or add a competing safety problem without addressing anything real.
- Treating the case as closed once "something" has been done, when the underlying uncertainty about what's actually happening remains unresolved.
- Diffusing ownership by adding activity across several fronts instead of first identifying who owns closing the formulation gap.

Ownership
The immediate owner is whoever holds the case (or the discussion facilitator/team lead) — their job now is to commission the missing upstream analysis (PORF/Suicide Enquiry/Contact Pattern Analysis/CRG Triage as relevant), not to select an intervention. Intervention selection re-engages once one of those produces a specific conclusion.

Review
Set a short, explicit deadline for the upstream formulation work to be completed (e.g., before the next case discussion or within a defined number of days) rather than leaving "something needs doing" open-ended. If established danger emerges at any point during that work, immediate safety action applies and bypasses this queue entirely — that is the one condition under which "we need to act now" is self-justifying.

Residual uncertainty
Everything about the actual clinical picture is still unknown: whether risk state has changed, whether a contact pattern is reinforcing something, whether an unmet need exists, and whether the current care/triage pathway is even mismatched. What is known is only that staff feel unease and have discussed the case at length — and that feeling, however legitimate as a prompt to look closer, is not itself a diagnosis.
