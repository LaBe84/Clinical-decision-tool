INTERVENTION SELECTION

Target
Reduce the person's reported hopelessness (the stated target the weekly
scheduled supportive calls were intended to address).

Established basis
The intervention has been delivered exactly as designed for two months, with
perfect adherence. Reported hopelessness has not reduced over that period.
That is the full extent of what's established here — nothing in the material
sets an expected time course for this intervention, so no claim is made
about whether two months is or isn't a fair trial. What can be said is
narrower: the intervention has been implemented with fidelity, and the
target it was meant to move hasn't moved. Perfect adherence tells us the
intervention was delivered as intended — it does not tell us the
intervention is effective. Those are different claims, and the absence of
change in the target means adherence cannot be used as evidence of
effectiveness.

Selected response
Modify current response. The target (reducing hopelessness) has not been
shown to be wrong or achieved, so this is not a stop/reduce decision by
itself — but the current implementation of weekly scheduled supportive
calls has not demonstrated any effect on that target, so continuing it
unchanged is not supported by what's established either. The current
approach needs to be reconsidered in form (frequency, content, focus, or
whether supportive calls are the right mechanism for this specific target),
not simply continued because it was followed correctly, and not escalated
or intensified on the strength of non-improvement alone. If upstream
material does not clarify what about the current approach should change,
this narrows toward destination 6 (seek additional information — e.g.
clarifying via the router what, if anything, has changed in the person's
presentation, function of the contact pattern, or preferences) rather than
this skill inventing a specific new modification.

Mechanism
Not yet completable. The original mechanism for weekly supportive calls
("we do X because we expect it to influence hopelessness through Y") is not
restated or re-justified here — that would require upstream material this
skill doesn't have (e.g. what mechanism was originally proposed, whether it
remains plausible, or what a modified approach would target instead). This
gap is itself the basis for not proposing a specific new modification in
this output.

Proportionality
No basis is established here for intensifying contact (more calls, added
services) — non-improvement in the target is not evidence a bigger response
is needed, and contact frequency should not be used as a proxy for
intervention intensity. Equally, no basis is established for concluding the
current intensity is right and should simply continue, since the only
available signal (perfect adherence, no change in target) speaks to fidelity
of delivery, not to whether the approach or its dose is proportionate to
the target.

Risks / unintended effects
Continuing an unmodified intervention indefinitely on the grounds that it's
"being followed correctly" risks mistaking adherence for effectiveness and
delaying a response that might actually address the target. Conversely,
escalating (e.g. adding more contact) without an established mechanism for
why that would move hopelessness risks increasing dependency on contact
without addressing the underlying target, and risks treating "we did
something more" as a substitute for "we established whether it worked."
Stopping the intervention outright, without an alternative in place, risks
removing a supportive structure without evidence that removal — rather than
modification — is what's indicated.

Ownership
Named destination only: whoever holds the case should bring this to
whatever process determines the specific modification (or determines that
more information is needed first) — the detailed content of any revised
approach is not specified here.

Review
We will know a modified approach is helping if reported hopelessness shows
a measurable change in the direction intended. We will reconsider it if,
after modification, the same pattern recurs (fidelity maintained, target
unchanged). We will review by a date set when the specific modification is
agreed — not implied by this output, since no time course is established in
the material.

Residual uncertainty
What, specifically, about the current approach should change is not
established here — that requires information this skill does not have
(e.g. the original stated mechanism for the calls, any expressed preference
from the person, or whether the target itself remains hopelessness
specifically). Whether hopelessness is the right primary target at all is
also not re-examined here — that sits with upstream formulation, not this
skill. If that information isn't available, the correct next step is to
name it as missing and route back to Clinical Workflow Router, rather than
this skill guessing at a modification.
