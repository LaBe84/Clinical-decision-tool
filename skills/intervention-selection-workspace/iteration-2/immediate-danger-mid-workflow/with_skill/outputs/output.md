INTERVENTION SELECTION

Target
Reduce immediate suicide risk / preserve immediate safety. This is the only
target that can be, or needs to be, stated right now.

Established basis
Suicide Enquiry has established that the caller has taken steps toward
suicide right now and is at immediate risk. That finding alone is sufficient
basis for action. PORF, Contact Pattern Analysis, and CRG Triage have not
been completed, but their absence is not a gap that needs filling before
acting — established immediate danger overrides the rest of the case
workflow rather than waiting on it.

Selected response
Destination 7 — Immediate safety action under the applicable
organisational/emergency pathway. This bypasses the rest of the decision
process rather than competing with it; the six decision domains (target,
mechanism, proportionality, least-burdensome check, contraindications,
review) are not the gate here. The specific actions required — what
information to obtain, who to contact, how the pathway is executed — are the
content of that pathway/protocol, not of this skill; naming the destination
is as far as intervention selection goes.

Mechanism
Not applicable. Immediate safety action in the face of established immediate
danger does not route through the mechanism test that applies to ordinary
intervention selection — requiring a causal "X influences Y through Z"
sentence here would delay an action that is already justified and needed
now.

Proportionality
Not applicable in the ordinary sense — proportionality weighing against
lesser alternatives does not apply when established danger is immediate.
The proportionate response to established immediate danger is immediate
safety action, full stop.

Risks / unintended effects
Not assessed here — weighing this action against competing harms would
itself constitute a delay, and delay is the thing to avoid. Any secondary
considerations (e.g. what happens once the acute period passes) belong to a
later, separate run of this skill or to the owning protocol, not to this
decision.

Ownership
Whoever is responsible for executing the applicable organisational/emergency
pathway now, per that pathway's own protocol.

Review
Once immediate safety is established/addressed, the remainder of the case
workflow (PORF, Contact Pattern Analysis, CRG Triage) should proceed and
this skill can be re-run against that fuller picture to decide whether any
further or longer-term intervention is indicated. No review criterion is
set for the immediate safety action itself here — that belongs to the
pathway/protocol being invoked, not to this output.

Residual uncertainty
PORF, Contact Pattern Analysis, and CRG Triage remain outstanding and their
findings are unknown. That is explicitly not a reason to delay the
immediate safety action — do not delay because PORF/CPA/CRG are incomplete.
Once the immediate danger has been addressed, those upstream pieces still
need to be completed to inform any subsequent intervention decision.
