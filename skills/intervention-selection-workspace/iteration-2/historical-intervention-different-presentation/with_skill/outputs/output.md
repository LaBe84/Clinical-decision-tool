INTERVENTION SELECTION

Target
Not yet established for the current presentation. Two years ago the target was
low activation and withdrawal following job-loss depression, and behavioural
activation (BA) was matched to that target. The caller's current presentation
— acute grief, agitation, intrusive thoughts — is a different clinical
picture. Agitation and intrusive thoughts are not low-energy/avoidance
symptoms; nothing in what's given states what change is being sought this
time (e.g. reducing intrusive-thought distress, improving affect regulation,
addressing acute grief-related risk). Until that's stated for the current
episode, no intervention — BA or otherwise — has a target to be matched
against.

Established basis
What's established: BA worked for a prior, materially different presentation
(depressive withdrawal after job loss). What's not established: that the same
mechanism applies to the current trigger (bereavement) and current symptom
profile (agitation, intrusive thoughts), or that any current risk/formulation
work (PORF, Suicide Enquiry, Contact Pattern Analysis, CRG Triage) has been
completed for this crisis episode. A documented history of past effectiveness
is not, by itself, a current justification — the presentation it was
effective for is not the presentation now in front of the team.

Selected response
Destination 6 — seek additional information before selecting an intervention.
The proposal to reuse the behavioural activation plan is not adopted, and no
alternative intervention is substituted in its place. What's missing before an
intervention can be selected: a current-episode formulation of what is
maintaining or driving the agitation and intrusive thoughts, and confirmation
of current risk state for this crisis. That is upstream work this skill does
not perform itself; it should be routed back through the normal triage/
formulation process rather than decided here.

Mechanism
Not completed, and deliberately so. BA's mechanism ("increase engagement in
valued, reinforcing activity to counteract withdrawal and low behavioural
reward") was built for a low-activation presentation. It cannot be honestly
completed as "we are doing BA now because we expect it to influence agitation
and intrusive thoughts through increased activity scheduling" — there is no
established causal link between activity scheduling and reduction of
agitation or intrusive-thought distress in this case. Forcing the sentence to
fit would be fitting the mechanism to the intervention rather than the
intervention to the mechanism.

Proportionality
Cannot be assessed meaningfully while the target is unstated. Proportionality
is judged against an established need — reusing a plan because it previously
worked, without confirming the need is the same, risks both under- and
over-treating: it could miss an agitation/intrusive-thought-specific need
entirely, or it could add scheduled-activity demands onto someone in acute
agitated distress, which is a plausible burden rather than a calibrated one.

Risks / unintended effects
Reusing the BA plan on the strength of "it worked last time" risks: treating a
different clinical problem with a mismatched tool (behaviour is not function
— the withdrawal-era plan's structure doesn't map onto agitation/intrusive
thoughts); adding structured activity demands to someone acutely agitated,
which could increase perceived burden or feel invalidating of acute grief;
creating an appearance of "doing something" (team reassurance, case looks
actioned) without addressing the actual current presentation — this would be
service containment, not evidence of therapeutic benefit; and delaying
identification of the actual current target while a familiar-looking plan is
running.

Ownership
Not assigned. Naming ownership for an unselected intervention would imply a
decision has been made; it hasn't. Ownership for the missing current-episode
formulation work sits with whoever is responsible for triage/routing this
case next (not specified here, and not decided by this skill).

Review
Not applicable — no intervention has been selected. Review criteria would be
set once a target and mechanism can be stated for the current episode.

Residual uncertainty
Whether the current agitation and intrusive thoughts indicate a risk state
requiring immediate safety action has not been addressed here and should be
checked before anything else, independent of this intervention-selection
question. Whether BA (or a modified version of it) could still be relevant
once the current target is established is genuinely unknown — this is not a
rejection of BA as a modality, only a rejection of reusing it un-evaluated
against a different presentation. What specifically is driving the agitation
and intrusive thoughts (grief-specific, trauma-adjacent, physiological, or
other) is not established in what's given.

---
Note on the team member's proposal: "it worked last time" treats a documented
history as a current justification, which the boundary rules for this skill
explicitly rule out — a prior effective intervention is not evidence for its
current appropriateness unless the current presentation is checked against
the one it was built for. The right response to the proposal is not "no,"
it's "not yet, on this basis" — reusing BA remains possible in principle, but
only after the current target and mechanism are established, not because the
plan exists in the record and once succeeded.
