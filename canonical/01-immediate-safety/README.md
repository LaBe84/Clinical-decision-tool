# Immediate Safety

This area will hold the canonical relationship between immediate safety action and the clinical reasoning workflow.

Initial proposition already reflected across the current skills/router:

> When the material establishes an acute, unfolding safety or medical emergency requiring action now, the applicable organisational emergency procedure takes precedence over routine skill routing.

The canonical layer will define the principle and authority boundary. It will not invent operational emergency procedures; those must come from applicable organisational policy/SOP.
