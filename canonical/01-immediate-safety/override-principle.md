# Immediate safety override — canonical draft

**Status:** DRAFT. The precedence principle is retained; the exact emergency and safeguarding procedure must come from current organisational policy.

When information indicates a current immediate safety concern, responding through the applicable emergency/safeguarding pathway takes precedence over normal skill sequencing, planning completion, access arrangements, and routing.

This is a precedence rule, not an operational protocol. Skills must neither delay immediate action for assessment/formulation documentation nor invent service-specific steps, thresholds, or destinations. A CMP/access arrangement cannot block or delay an applicable immediate-safety response.
