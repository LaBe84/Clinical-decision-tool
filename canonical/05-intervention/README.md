# Intervention

This area will hold cross-cutting intervention principles and canonical models that are consumed by intervention-design/review skills.

Initial domains:

- Safety Planning
- CMP / access arrangements

Key boundary: intervention must follow the established clinical problem/formulation. Operational pressure can establish that a service problem requires attention, but does not by itself determine the clinically appropriate intervention.

Safety planning must remain distinct from assessment/formulation. CMP/access arrangements must remain distinct from punishment, convenience-driven restriction or assumptions based on contact volume alone.
