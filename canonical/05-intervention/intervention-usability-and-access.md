# Intervention usability and access — canonical draft

**Status:** DRAFT. Safety-planning model requirements and CMP/access rules require source and governance verification.

## Safety planning
Safety planning is a collaborative intervention built from established enquiry and formulation; it does not recreate assessment or formulate risk. A plan's existence, agreement, or apparent completeness is not evidence that it is usable in the actual crisis state. Test person-specific actions, resource availability, feasibility, collaboration status, and what remains unknown. A safety plan must not disguise an access restriction.

## CMP/access review
A CMP/access arrangement is considered as a clinical intervention, not merely a response to volume, staff burden, or difficult behaviour. Review clinical purpose, proportionality, actual effects, and whether the arrangement remains workable. High contact, unknown function, staff emotional burden, a single breach, compliance, or an existing CMP do not independently justify tighter restriction or prove effectiveness.

Least-restrictive workable access and the role of evidenced service-delivery impact are organisational-rule candidates, not approved policy. Immediate safety remains available regardless of CMP terms.
