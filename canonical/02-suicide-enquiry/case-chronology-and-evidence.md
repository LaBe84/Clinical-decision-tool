# Suicide enquiry: chronology and evidence — canonical draft

**Status:** DRAFT. CASE source/version and exact time-horizon terminology require controlled verification.

Suicide enquiry records what has been directly established, organised by the adopted chronology: current/immediate, recent, lifetime, and current/next anticipated information.

- Direct enquiry is not replaced by inference from tone, mood, history, or contact behaviour.
- Record the question, response, relevant own words, and unresolved gaps where available.
- Ambiguous death-related language is not categorised until its meaning is clarified.
- Enquiry does not assign a risk level, score, formulation conclusion, or operational destination.
- Information disclosed during enquiry that indicates immediate danger is acted on without waiting for the enquiry output to be complete.

Exact labels such as denied, not disclosed, and not established are reasoning distinctions, not yet approved organisational documentation terminology.
