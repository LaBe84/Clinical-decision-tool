# Escalation, oversight and decision locus — canonical draft

**Status:** DRAFT. Helpline, TL/Safety Huddle, and CRG destination rules require an approved current service source.

Risk-based escalation and oversight/continuity escalation are different decisions. Persisting inability to establish a clinically material picture can justify coordinated review without proving elevated risk.

Escalation follows an unresolved, defined decision: for example, a material uncertainty that routine management cannot safely contain, a coordination/visibility problem requiring real-time agreement, or a formulation/intervention that no longer accounts for the presentation. It does not follow from complexity, frequency, high contact, staff anxiety, elevated distress, or incomplete information alone.

“Insufficient information to triage” is an honest output state, not a destination. No exact service destination, threshold, or mandatory workflow in this draft is approved until verified against organisational policy/remit.
