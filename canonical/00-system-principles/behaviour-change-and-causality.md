# Behaviour, change and causality — canonical draft

**Status:** DRAFT local reasoning discipline. Operational data definitions and organisational rules remain subject to verification.

- Observed behaviour is not inferred function, motive, diagnosis, or risk.
- Frequency, contact volume, intensity of distress, compliance, or a single boundary event do not independently establish clinical meaning.
- Reduced contact is not evidence of improvement; increased contact is not evidence of deterioration; non-contact/loss of visibility is loss of information, not proof of worsening.
- A stable pattern is not evidence that an intervention is effective.
- Coexistence over time does not establish that a response is causing, reinforcing, or maintaining a pattern.
- Change must be considered against an established baseline where one exists. Without one, describe current indicators and the limitation; do not assert change from an unknown starting point.
- Separate observation, supported inference, hypothesis, and unresolved question in all outputs.
