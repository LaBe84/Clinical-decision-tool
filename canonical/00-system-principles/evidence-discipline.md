# Evidence Discipline

Status: **DRAFT CANONICAL — local reasoning invariant, not organisational policy**

## Core rules

1. **Unknown is not negative evidence.** Missing, unasked, unavailable or unseen information remains unknown unless the material establishes otherwise.
2. **Observation and interpretation must remain distinguishable.** Record what happened before stating what it may mean.
3. **Behaviour does not establish function.** Frequency, duration, disengagement, reassurance seeking, calmness, compliance or breach are observable behaviours. Function/motive requires evidence or formulation.
4. **Do not manufacture completion.** A blank conceptual domain can be completed honestly with 'not established' or equivalent uncertainty language.
5. **Do not convert loss of visibility into deterioration or improvement.** Reduced contact, failed contact or disengagement changes what is known; it does not itself establish direction of clinical change.
6. **Do not infer causal maintenance from temporal coexistence alone.** A service response and a repeating pattern occurring together can raise a formulation question, but do not by themselves show that one is reinforcing the other.
7. **Do not resolve inconsistency by averaging it away.** Conflicting accounts should remain visible with provenance/time where material.
8. **A direct negative answer is data, not a global conclusion.** It should not silently become reassurance outside the domain actually asked about.

## Provenance discipline

Where relevant, distinguish:

- directly reported / observed;
- collateral;
- historical;
- inferred;
- unknown / not established.

## Application

These rules apply across Suicide Enquiry, PORF, Contact Pattern Analysis, CRG Triage, CMP/Access Review, Safety Planning and the workflow router.

Skill-specific wording may differ, but it must not weaken these evidence boundaries.
