# Authority, uncertainty and reasoning boundaries — canonical draft

**Status:** DRAFT. This file records cross-system reasoning discipline. It does not establish organisational procedure or thresholds.

## Authority
Applicable organisational policy and mandatory procedure take precedence over canonical drafts and skills. A skill must not manufacture a policy requirement, destination, threshold, or operational procedure where the authoritative source is absent.

## Uncertainty
- Unknown, unasked, unavailable, and not established are not negative findings.
- Record observed/directly reported, collateral, historical, inferred, and unknown material distinctly.
- A direct denial, non-disclosure, and an unestablished domain are different evidential states; exact documentation labels remain local-design candidates pending verification.
- Conflicting accounts are retained with their source and timing; they are not averaged into a false certainty.
- A downstream capability must name and route an information gap, not fill it in to complete its output.

## Boundaries
Suicide Enquiry establishes information; PORF interprets its clinical significance; intervention skills apply established material; escalation resolves a defined decision at the appropriate level. Completion of documentation is not evidence that the underlying reasoning is adequate.
