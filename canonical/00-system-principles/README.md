# Canonical System Principles

This directory contains cross-cutting reasoning principles that are authoritative across multiple skills once reviewed and approved.

A principle belongs here when it describes how evidence, uncertainty, behaviour, change, authority or reasoning must be handled across the system. Skill-specific procedural instructions belong in the relevant skill instead.

## Initial principles to formalise

- Evidence discipline: absence of supplied evidence is not evidence of absence.
- Behaviour and function: observed behaviour does not establish its function or motive.
- Change and baseline: increases/decreases in contact or apparent stability require interpretation against baseline and function.
- Uncertainty: uncertainty must remain visible and must not be silently converted into reassurance or escalation.
- Authority boundaries: each skill must stay within its defined task and route missing upstream/downstream work rather than absorbing it.

These are scaffolds pending source/provenance review. They must not be treated as newly approved organisational policy merely because they appear in `canonical/`.
