# Policies and Mandatory Procedures

This directory is reserved for authoritative organisational policy/procedure sources or controlled references to them.

Policy is not to be reconstructed from an AI skill, an eval case or historical practice.

For each policy source, record at minimum:

- title;
- issuing organisation/service;
- version/date;
- status/currentness;
- source location;
- relevant mandatory requirements;
- relationship to canonical rules and skills.

Where policy conflicts with a skill or locally defined canonical rule, applicable organisational policy takes precedence and the conflict should be surfaced for review rather than silently reconciled.
