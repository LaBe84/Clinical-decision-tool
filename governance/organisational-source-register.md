## Rules before operational reliance

- Canonical content may preserve high-level uncertainty and source deferral; it must not infer unrecorded thresholds, workflow steps, exceptions or destinations.
- Where organisational sources conflict or remain materially ambiguous, canonical content must preserve that uncertainty and defer the matter to the accountable organisational owner rather than resolve it by inference.
- If a relied-on source is withdrawn, superseded, materially amended or subsequently found not to be the authoritative controlled version, affected canonical rules revert to provisional status pending impact review and reapproval.
- A source title, filename, summary or prior circulation is not evidence that a canonical rule is organisationally approved. Operational reliance requires verification against the current controlled source and an accountable approval decision.
